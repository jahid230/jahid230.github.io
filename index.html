03 Current project
Bakbakum Paediatric speech & listening rehabilitation · in Gründung · active development, limited clinical pilot

A therapist-controlled, parent-implemented platform for children with speech sound disorders and for cochlear-implant recipients in post-implant rehabilitation. One principle runs through it: the clinician authors the plan, the system executes it at home.

A speech-language pathologist writes a structured care plan in a clinician dashboard. At home, the child practises with a friendly character through short, playful spoken exercises that weave the therapist's target words into the conversation. What happened in the session goes back to the therapist, who adjusts the plan for the next cycle.

I left Experian at the end of November 2025 and have been building it since January 2026 as co-founder. The venture is pre-incorporation and pre-revenue: nothing is registered, nothing is sold, and under the EU MDR nothing may be placed on the market before CE marking. My role in the team is the technical engineering: I own the architecture and the implementation across the child-facing app, the clinical system of record, the real-time speech runtime, and the EU data-protection work that a paediatric health product needs before it can touch a real clinic.
